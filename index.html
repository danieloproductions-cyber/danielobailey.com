About Me

Daniel O' Bailey — also published as Daniel Bailey — is a writer, editor, interviewer, musician, and communications producer from Madisonville, Louisiana.

His work sits where editorial judgment meets field work: interviews, research, public-facing copy, narrative nonfiction, music, and creative production. He is interested in stories that make complex people and ideas legible without sanding off their human grain.

For employers and collaborators, the practical value is simple: Daniel can interview, research, draft, edit, coordinate, produce, revise, and deliver under pressure while keeping the voice alive and the facts clean.
